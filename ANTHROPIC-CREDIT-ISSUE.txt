ERROR: "Your credit balance is too low to access the Anthropic API"

✅ SOLUTIONS:

OPTION 1: ADD PAYMENT METHOD (RECOMMENDED)
────────────────────────────────────────

1. Go to: https://console.anthropic.com/account/billing/overview
2. Click "Plans & Billing"
3. Add payment method (credit card)
4. Choose plan:
   • Pay-as-you-go (starts at $5)
   • Free trial ($5-10 initial credits)
5. After adding payment, you can use API immediately
6. Cost: ~$0.001-0.005 per chat

OPTION 2: WAIT FOR MONTHLY RESET
────────────────────────────────

If you had free trial credits:
• They expire after 3 months
• Or when you use them up
• Can't reuse trial
• Need to add payment method

OPTION 3: CHECK YOUR ACCOUNT STATUS
──────────────────────────────────

1. Go to: https://console.anthropic.com/account/billing/overview
2. Check "Account balance"
3. Look at "Usage this month"
4. See remaining credits
